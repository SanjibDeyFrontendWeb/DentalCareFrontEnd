FAQ

Common questions

Still unsure? Call us on +91 98300 12345.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. They are indicative starting prices. Your dentist confirms the exact plan and cost after examining you — and you always approve it before treatment starts.

Is root canal treatment painful?

With modern local anaesthesia and rotary instruments, most patients feel little more than a normal filling. We check your comfort throughout.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Our pediatric appointments are relaxed and child-friendly, and parents are welcome in the room.

Can I pay in instalments?

For larger treatments such as implants and aligners we can discuss EMI options, subject to eligibility.
